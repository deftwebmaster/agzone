    Photo: Cho jaesun, CC BY-SA 3.0

    Our focus

    Small tanks, specialist gear

    Nano aquariums and shrimp tanks are some of the most rewarding setups in the hobby — and some of the least forgiving. Small water volumes change quickly, tiny livestock can be lost to an unprotected filter intake, and the right tools make planting and maintenance far easier.

    AquaGearZone brings that specialist equipment together in one place: nano tanks and lids, gentle shrimp-safe filtration, aquascaping tools, lighting and the maintenance gear that keeps a small aquarium stable. Every product is presented the same way, so you can compare specifications, suitable setups and limitations at a glance.

    How we work

    Our editorial principles

    • Documented, not exaggerated

      Product information is compiled from manufacturer documentation and specifications. We don't claim to have tested equipment unless we actually have, and we say so on the page when we do.

    • Limitations included

      Every product lists limitations alongside its strengths — sizing constraints, maintenance needs and situations where it isn't the right choice.

    • No invented numbers

      We don't publish star ratings, fake reviews, sale prices or stock levels. Current pricing and availability are always shown by the retailer.

    • Livestock first

      Guides follow widely published husbandry guidance and favour stability and gentleness over shortcuts.

    Transparency

    How AquaGearZone is funded

    AquaGearZone doesn't sell products, hold inventory or take payments. Our “View at Retailer” buttons send you to established online stores, where you complete your purchase.

    When retailer partnerships are in place, some of those links will be affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy — at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships never determine whether a product's limitations are shown. Read the full affiliate disclosure.
