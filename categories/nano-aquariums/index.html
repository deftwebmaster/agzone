    Nano Aquariums

    Nano aquariums — usually anything under about 10 gallons — are a popular way to start in the hobby, but small water volumes leave less margin for error. The equipment here focuses on stable, well-supported setups: properly leveled glass, lids that limit evaporation, and kits with filtration sized for the tank.
